# Company / Domain Context

FAMS (Fuel Automation Management Systems) provides fuel tank gauging and
dispensing automation. This skill audits the integrity of fuel dispensing
transaction data captured across ATG (Automatic Tank Gauge), IOT devices,
and Android handheld loggers, for customer accounts spanning industries
such as mining, logistics, agriculture, municipalities, fuel suppliers, and
manufacturing (see `examples/`).
